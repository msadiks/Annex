# Code of Conduct

Annex follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the issues, pull requests and anywhere else you represent the
project.

In short:

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent; most people here help in their
  spare time, and many write in a second language.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexual, discriminatory or
  violent language or imagery.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
[@msadiks](https://github.com/msadiks), privately using the contact details
on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these
rules, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does,
following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
