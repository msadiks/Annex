Pair with your PC

On the PC, open the Annex tray menu and choose Pair with a Mac… Then type the PIN it shows.

From now on both apps reconnect by themselves whenever they’re on the same network.

Can’t see your PC? Both computers must be on the same network, and Windows Firewall must allow Annex on private networks (TCP 48800, UDP 48801).
